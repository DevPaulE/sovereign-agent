## 1. The numbers (say these)

- **~82%** — drop in profit-per-worker over the four years after an owner's unexpected death. *That* is the key-person discount every buyer prices in. (Everywhere Millionaire, Zwick et al.)
- **The seller's cost:** a business that would sell for X sells for 0.6–0.8X — or not at all — when it lives in the owner's head.
- **The broker's cost:** their fee is **tied to enterprise value**. A 20% higher multiple on a $1M business is **$20k+ more commission** for them. The data room is not a favour to the seller; it is the broker's own upside.
- **The build:** a dæmon-produced data room in **30 days, $2,500**.
- **The hardware proof:** a real model stack doing real work on **under $5,000** of hardware, **on the owner's premises**. Their books never leave the building.

## 2. The checklist (the gate — and the demo)

The buyer's real diligence set (`bigjoecoding/smb-acquisition-diligence`, 4 phases):

| Phase | What the buyer runs |
|---|---|
| **1 Screening** | asking price & SDE sanity, reason for sale, go/no-go |
| **2 LOI** | attorney/CPA engaged, SBA pre-qual, **full doc request list** |
| **3 Deep diligence** | **Financial** (3–5 yr P&L vs tax returns, SDE add-backs sourced) · **Legal** (entity, contracts, assignability, UCC liens) · **Operational** (key-person dependency, documented systems) · **Customer** (top-5/10 concentration) · **HR** (W-2 v 1099) · **Facilities** (lease assignability) · **Regulatory/Env** · **Insurance** · **IT/Data security** |
| **4 Closing** | purchase agreement, transition plan, non-compete, financing cleared |

**The demo is the gap report:** *"Here's the checklist your buyer will run. Here's what the dæmon verified. Here's what's missing. Decide."* One page. It IS the sales page.

## 3. The questions to ask (let them do the work)

1. **"Of your current listings, how many would fail the buyer's diligence checklist today?"** — the whole pitch in one question.
2. **"What does that cost them at closing?"** — the money number, in their words.
3. **"How much of your week goes to chasing documents that should already exist?"**
4. **"Would a data room that arrives verified raise your multiple — or just close faster?"**

Listen for **their** words. That answer *is* the story. (Research first, format second.)

## 4. The offer

> **"Buyers discount what they can't verify — and after 30 years, most of your business lives in your head and your inbox. I install a dæmon that turns it into a diligence-ready data room in 30 days, for $2,500 — so a buyer prices the business you built, not the paperwork you didn't."**

**Tiers:** (1) the **owner-dependence audit** (small fixed fee, one page) → (2) the **data room** ($2,500, 30 days) → (3) the **owner-independence dossier** (the exit-value artifact). Start where they say yes.

- *"We use Firmex."* → "Firmex is the **storage**. I'm the **production** — the dæmon fills it, verified against the checklist. Keep Firmex; I make it complete."
