## THE ANALYSIS

Analyze my work in this record by showing me three parallel universes.

### UNIVERSE A — The Amateur Version
Rewrite exactly what happened if this had been handled by someone with **one year of experience** — an AI-curious marketer or junior developer.

For each major moment:
- Where they'd get stuck or confused
- The mistakes they'd make (and why those seem logical to a beginner)
- The opportunities they'd completely miss
- The inferior outcome (value lost, timeline extended, decisions damaged)
- **What I made look easy that would have been hard for them**

### UNIVERSE B — The Different Expert Approaches
Show how three experts would handle the same record. Choose from adjacent but distinct fields:
1. **A Big-4 / McKinsey consultant** (framework-first, deliverable-shaped)
2. **A staff software engineer** (system-first, correctness-shaped)
3. **A startup founder / GTM lead** (market-first, revenue-shaped)

For each:
- How their training shapes the approach
- What they'd focus on that I didn't (and why that matters less here)
- What I caught or prioritized that they'd completely miss
- Where their "best practices" would produce a **worse** outcome in this context
- **What I know about THIS specific context that their general expertise wouldn't cover**

### UNIVERSE C — The AI Limitation Analysis
What could even an advanced AI not replicate about my approach, even with perfect instructions?
- **Judgment calls** — decisions requiring lived experience over logic
- **Intuitive leaps** — what I "just knew" from pattern recognition
- **Context reading** — cues I picked up that need presence
- **Adaptive expertise** — where I broke my own rules because the situation called for it
- **Relationship intelligence** — human dynamics not reducible to a framework
- **Timing and pacing** — knowing when to push, wait, or redirect

## SYNTHESIS — The Uniqueness Formula

1. **The Expertise Stack** — what creates my unique value:
   - *Surface skills* (anyone can learn these)
   - *Deep patterns* (take years)
   - *Irreplaceable judgment* (possibly unteachable)
2. **The Competitive Moat** — what breaks if someone copies my surface approach without the deep knowledge
3. **The Hidden ROI** — value created that wasn't visible at the time:
   - Problems prevented that never surfaced
   - Future complications avoided
   - Mental models installed
   - Confidence built in others
4. **The Packageable Frameworks** — 3–5 frameworks, decision trees, or diagnostics I'm using unconsciously, each worth **$5K–$50K** as standalone IP:
   - Name it
   - The problem it solves
   - Why it's valuable (evidence from the universes)
   - What someone would pay for it
