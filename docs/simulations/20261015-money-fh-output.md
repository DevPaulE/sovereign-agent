### Operating Level Dial

**Level 1: Manual / Direct Execution.**
You have zero paying clients. You do not have an automated sales funnel, a validated pitch, or a proven delivery benchmark on a client machine. Operating at Level 2+ (delegating outreach to agents or building automated pipelines) is stacking leverage on top of unvalidated assumptions. You do the outreach, the calls, the audits, and the installs by hand until Client #1 pays and clears acceptance.

---

### 1. Archetype Proposals

#### Ray Kroc (Systems & Replication)
- Sell the audit as an intake assembly line: input must be strictly one folder (`/inbox`), output strictly one markdown page.
- Scope the $5k install to a single pinned template (one specific trigger, one specific deliverable, no bespoke sprawl).
- Do not touch Manila or custom family setups; they break standard operating procedure and don't yield repeatable case studies.
- Price compute hosting day one ($500/mo retainer) so the asset creates recurring enterprise value, not just one-off cash.
- Measure only one metric: cycle time from lead receipt to deployed deliverable.

#### The Founder (Sell Before You Build, Do Things That Don't Scale)
- The warm list of 25 is currently a procrastination shield; send personalized messages to all 25 today manually.
- The Uncle in Manila is your fastest path to a live production deployment; ship it this weekend for proof, not money.
- On discovery calls, do not explain the tech; ask where they bleed 10 hours a week and manually solve it first.
- Offer a risk-free deal to the first qualified warm lead: "$2.5k down, $2.5k when you verify the 20 hours saved."
- Do whatever unscalable manual labor is necessary behind the scenes to make the agent look magic on Day 14.

#### Don Draper (The Narrative Hook)
- The hook is not efficiency; it is sovereign control and quiet relief from operational chaos.
- Frame the pitch around the vulnerability: "Your business secrets are feeding Silicon Valley's models while you drown in administrative sludge."
- Position the $5k install as an executive asset: a personal machine that works only for them, stays in their physical room, and forgets nothing.
- The free audit must hurt: it shouldn't just list automation opportunities; it must expose the exact dollar cost of owner-dependency.
- Make signing an act of status: "I only take two installations a month because sovereign systems cannot be mass-produced."

#### The Conversation Engineer (Narrative + Verified Artifact)
- Anchor every interaction to a verified artifact: the one-page audit with exact file citations proves competence without claims.
- The offer is an airtight contract: "$5k fixed, 14 days, 20+ hours recovered, verified against your baseline numbers."
- Build the proof ledger live: document every task the agent takes over with logged timestamps and before/after metrics.
- Keep the language disciplined: eliminate "agents/AI/FDE"; speak entirely in hours reclaimed, data retained, and deliverable SLAs.
- Turn the completed installation into a publishable work order and teardown that closes leads 2 through 5.

#### The Mirror (Sagittarius Rising / Virgo 10th Stellium)
- **Sagittarius Rising** wants the grand architectural vision: the sovereign multi-node lab, the manifesto, the future of work.
- **Virgo 10th Stellium** is where the money lives: rigorous craft, obsessive precision, hands in the engine, proof in the ledger.
- Trap to kill: using Sagittarius vision-building (tweaking the M6 stack, refining theories) to avoid the granular discomfort of sales outreach.
- Rhyme with the chart: sell the big sovereign vision (Sagittarius) through an undeniably meticulous, surgically verified audit artifact (Virgo).
- Grounding rule: zero architecture work on the lab until outbound messages are sent. Craft serves commerce, not vice versa.

### 2. The Debate: 3 Real Disagreements

1. **The Uncle Pilot: Fast Lab vs. Waste of Time**
   - *The Founder* wants the Manila deployment live immediately to prove the stack in real conditions and build momentum.
   - *Kroc* rejects it: family in Manila with non-standard needs produces zero commercial signal, zero cash, and breaks standard delivery patterns.
2. **Pricing Structure: Fixed Certainty vs. De-risked Closing**
   - *Kroc & Conversation Engineer* demand fixed $5,000 upfront to filter tire-kickers and fund standard delivery.
   - *The Founder* argues zero sales record requires a performance split ($2,500 down, $2,500 on verification) to eliminate buyer friction for Lead #1.
3. **The Audit Role: Diagnostic Pain vs. Standard Intake**
   - *Draper* insists the audit must dramatize owner vulnerability and risk to force an emotional buy decision.
   - *Kroc* insists the audit is simply a mechanical qualifying filter: pass the folder test or get dropped from the pipeline.

### 3. The Fused, Grounded Answer: Plan to Land Client #1

#### Week 1: Outbound, Qualification, and Uncle Proof
- **Uncle Pilot (Speed Run):** Deploy the uncle's task agent on M6 lab over 48 hours. Treat it as delivery drills: measure hours to deploy, document the before/after, and capture the first verified reference quote.
- **The Warm 25 Sprint:** Manually reach out to 5 names per day from the 25-person list (plain language, no jargon).
  - *Message angle:* "I’m running a diagnostic for business owners buried in admin: send me one messy operations folder, I send back a 1-page breakdown of your owner bottlenecks and the single task eating 15+ hours/month that can be handed to a local machine. No cost, 48-hour turnaround."
- **Target:** 5 audits booked/requested by Friday.

#### Week 2: Audits and Pitches
- Process returned folders through the Dæmon Audit engine (1 folder in, 1 page out, exact file citations, quantified cost of owner bottleneck).
- Deliver each audit over a 20-minute review call:
  - Page 1 walkthrough: show their data leaks and owner-dependency points.
  - The Offer: "$5,000 fixed, 14 days, I install a machine that takes over [Finding #1]. You get 20+ hours/week back, data stays in your office/lab, verified on your numbers."
  - De-risking term (Client #1 only): $2,500 deposit to start, $2,500 on signed acceptance of the 20-hour recovery threshold.
- **Target:** 1 signed engagement with deposit received.

#### Week 3: Delivery (14-Day Clock Starts)
- Day 1–3: Establish baseline metrics and pin the single deliverable.
- Day 4–10: Deploy the dæmon (M6 lab router/sentinel, scoped to their specific workflow).
- Day 11–14: Parallel run. Validate output accuracy and measure hours saved against baseline.

#### Week 4: Acceptance and Retainer Transition
- Run formal acceptance gate: client signs off on verified hours recovered. Collect second $2,500.
- Present compute hosting: "$500/month to keep the dæmon running on dedicated lab infrastructure with sentinel monitoring and daily memory consolidation."
- Package the engagement into an anonymized 1-page teardown/proof asset for warm leads 26–50.

### 5. The Falsifier

**If 25 personalized warm outreach messages yield fewer than 2 folder submissions for the free audit, the offer premise is falsified.** 
That result proves the pain hook ("recovered hours + sovereign data") is either improperly targeted, insufficiently urgent, or the warm list does not view you as the authority to solve it. In that event, halt all build work and pivot the diagnostic focus before contacting another lead.
