# FULL SEQUENCES — John and the uncle (Day 1 / 3 / 5 / 8)

> Dell's six-message / four-day cadence (`OUTREACH-DELL.md`), written out for the two names you can actually send to today. Rules held throughout: **goal = a reply, not a meeting** · 30–150 words · fifth-grade level · no ROI · no "AI/agents" · the ask is always smaller than a yes.

---

## SEQUENCE 1 — JOHN (cousin, SVAutoPros — the live client)

*This is not a sale. John already runs the system. The sequence asks for two things: his words, and one introduction.*

### Day 1 — the favor (email)
**Subject:** `Quick favor, John`

> Hey John — the shop bot's been running on your real Square data for a couple of months. Quick favor.
>
> You're the only person who's seen this thing work from the inside. I'm starting to show it to other shop owners, and the one thing I don't have is *your* words for it. Not mine.
>
> Two things, whenever you've got a minute:
> 1. One or two sentences I can quote — what it actually saves you.
> 2. One other owner you'd put me in front of.
>
> No rush at all.

### Day 1 — the DM (text/WhatsApp, +1 channel)
> Hey John — sent you a quick note. Nothing urgent, just wanted your words on the bot for a couple of other owners. Talk soon.

### Day 3 — the power forward
**Subject:** `(forward) Quick favor, John`

> John — bumping this up so it doesn't vanish under shop noise. No rush.
>
> Still just the two things: your sentence on what the bot saves you, and one owner you'd intro me to.

### Day 5 — a different angle, same theme
**Subject:** `The who-owes-me list`

> John — different thought. The first thing every owner asks me about is the who-owes-me list. You were the one who ran it on real Square data before anyone.
>
> That's the story I can't tell about myself. Worth capturing while it's fresh?

### Day 8 — the value-added close-out (lower the effort)
**Subject:** `Last note on this`

> John — last one on this, promise. If it's just a busy season, say so and I'll drop it.
>
> Easier option: I'll draft the two sentences myself and send them over — you just tell me if I got it right. And if no owner comes to mind, that's fine too.

**Stop.** Re-list in 20 days.

## SEQUENCE 2 — THE UNCLE (family, Manila — scheduling + tasks)

*The goal is one export folder and ten minutes. Everything else is a smaller version of that.*

### Day 1 — the direct insight (email / chat)
**Subject:** `Scheduling question`

> Hi `[uncle's name]` — you've got a calendar, a to-do list, and three chat threads, and none of them talk to each other. So every week starts from scratch.
>
> I've been building something for exactly that. It reads your schedule and your tasks, remembers them, and tells you what to do next, in order. It runs on my machine, so your stuff doesn't go out to anyone's cloud.
>
> I'd rather show you than explain it. Send me one export — your calendar, or a list of what's on your plate — and I'll send back what it makes of it. Free, no strings.

### Day 1 — the DM (WhatsApp — the uncle's channel, +1 channel)
> Tito `[name]` — sent you an email. Short version: I built something for the exact problem you described last time, the calendar-and-tasks juggling. Want to try it on your real stuff? Free, and it stays on my machine.

### Day 3 — the power forward
**Subject:** `(forward) Scheduling question`

> Tito — forwarding in case it got lost. The offer's just this: send me one export, I send back a one-page "what to do next." If it's useless, you'll know in five minutes.

### Day 5 — a different angle, same theme
**Subject:** `Nothing carries forward`

> Tito — the thing that actually bugs you isn't the number of tasks. It's that nothing carries forward. Every tool you use forgets by Monday.
>
> That's the one thing I fixed. Want to see it on your real week?

### Day 8 — the value-added close-out
**Subject:** `Not the right time?`

> Tito — is this just not the right week? Say the word and I'll leave it.
>
> If it's easier, just forward me one thing — your calendar export, or even a screenshot of your to-do list. I'll do the rest and send it back.

**Stop.** Re-list in 20 days.

## The rules for both (do not break)

- **No meeting ask.** The ask is an export, a sentence, or an intro.
- **No ROI numbers.** The skeptic brain rejects them — *you don't know me*.
- **No "AI," no "agents," no "FDE."** Hours recovered, data staying home.
- **Send the Day 1 email and the Day 1 DM the same day.** Dell: they won't switch channels on their own.
- **One spreadsheet or note per name**, so Day 3, 5, and 8 actually happen. A cadence you don't track is a wish.
