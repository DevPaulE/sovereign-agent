## LaCivita's resume structure (the assets to use)

**One page, six sections:**

```
NAME
contact line
CAREER PROFILE   →  [2-3 word descriptor] with [N] years in [profession]
                    within [company types]. [major accomplishment].
                    [one more achievement]. Core competencies include [f1, f2…].
CAREER HIGHLIGHTS →  three specific accomplishments (the power section)
PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
EDUCATION
EXTRACURRICULAR & VOLUNTEER
```

**The perfect bullet answers seven questions** — and this is the mechanism, not a style note:
1. the major benefit (**first**), 2. by how much, 3. from what to what, 4. from when to when, 5. what *you* specifically did, 6. who benefited, 7. context.

**The eight areas employers care about:** revenue · market awareness · customer attraction · customer happiness · company growth · employee happiness · cost reduction · **process efficiency**. (Paul's entire body of work lands in the last one, repeatedly.)

**The tooling:** the *Career Achievements Journal* (to surface accomplishments you've forgotten), the *Resume Content Builder*, the *Ultimate Resume Submittal Checklist*, and **jobscan.co** for ATS keyword matching before submitting.

## The letters and the interview

- **4-Sentence Cover Letter** (when an opening exists): why you contacted → who you are + what you offer + years/specialty/skills → the resume's three highlights → welcome the conversation.
- **7-Sentence "No-Job-Opening" Cover Letter** (when none does): inquire whether there's an opportunity → your research rationale → years/specialty/skills → **one home-run accomplishment** → the resume's highlights → welcome the conversation → thanks. *This is the letter for AI Fund's "Submit your Resume for Future Hiring."*
- **How to Interview the Employer (75 questions):** when the interviewer asks *"do you have any questions?"* they have handed you control of the interview. Prepare questions as a tool, not a formality.
- **The Criteria/Questions Matrix (XLSX):** 14 criteria — appreciation, atmosphere, autonomy, boss, challenge, commute, contribution, culture, flexibility, growth, people, role, travel (+ comp) — each with the questions that reveal it. **This is the direct answer to "am I getting stuck?": score the opportunity before accepting it, instead of feeling it out.**
