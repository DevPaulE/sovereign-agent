## The five drafted openers (real names, Dell's structure)

### 1 — John (cousin, SVAutoPros — the live client)
**Subject:** `Quick favor, John`

> Hey John — the shop bot's been running on your real Square data for a couple of months now. Quick favor.
>
> You're the only person who's seen this thing work from the inside. I'm starting to show it to other shop owners, and the one thing I don't have is *your* words for it. Not mine.
>
> Two things, whenever you've got a minute: (1) one or two sentences I can quote — what it actually saves you — and (2) is there one other owner you'd put me in front of?
>
> No rush at all.

*Why it works:* warm, no pitch, asks for a testimonial (proof) + one intro (the referral). Dell's "show me you know me" is effortless here.

### 2 — The uncle (family, Manila — scheduling + tasks)
**Subject:** `Scheduling question`

> Hi `[uncle's name]` — you've got a calendar, a to-do list, and three chat threads, and none of them talk to each other. So every week starts from scratch.
>
> I've been building something for exactly that: it reads your schedule and your tasks, remembers them, and tells you what to do next, in order. It runs on my machine, so your stuff doesn't go out to anyone's cloud.
>
> I'd rather show you than explain it. Send me one export and give me five minutes — I'll send back what it makes of it. Free, no strings.

*Why it works:* it's the uncle's own problem, honestly stated. The ask is an export, not a call.

### 3 — Colin Davis (peer — same ICP, opposite end)
**Subject:** `Same shop owners`

> Hi Colin — I found your work through Throttle Up. You coach the 2–10-person owner on the psychology and the systems; I build the machine that captures the work when the owner steps out. Same ICP, opposite ends.
>
> I don't want anything from you yet. I'd just like 20 minutes to compare notes on what the owners actually choke on — I suspect you see it before I do.
>
> Would you be open to that?

*Why it works:* peer to peer, no ask for business, a genuinely useful exchange. This is the *relationship* Dell talks about, not a sale.

### 4 — Kevin (civic contact — the Foster City avatar)
**Subject:** `That civic idea`

> Hi Kevin — you told me last year that people in local government want something impressive to show their constituents. I finally built the thing we talked about: an assistant that answers only from real city sources — candidate forums, ballot records — and cites every answer so nobody can accuse it of making things up.
>
> Would you be open to a look? And is there still someone at the city you'd introduce me to?

*Why it works:* it closes a loop Kevin opened. The ask is small and specific.

### 5 — A warm friend/family opener (the fill-in shape)
**Subject:** `What are you working on`

> Hi `[name]` — `[one true thing: a recent thing they posted / a move / a job change]`.
>
> I've been building something and I want a real reaction from someone I trust, not a pitch. It's a small local system that reads a folder of your work and hands back a one-page map — what's slowing you down, and the one thing to fix first. Runs on my machine; nothing goes to a cloud.
>
> What are you working on these days — anything that eats your week?

*Why it works:* it's a real question, not a sales email. It reopens the relationship and finds the trigger.
