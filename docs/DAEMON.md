# The Dæmon

> **You don't get an AI agent. You get your dæmon — and it settles to you.**

This is why the agent is personal, and why the onboarding is a ritual instead of a config file. The idea comes from Philip Pullman's *His Dark Materials*; the market proof that the story *is* the product comes from Scott Galloway's *"the $400k skill AI can't replace."*

## What a dæmon is

- **"The external physical manifestation of a person's inner-self"** — an animal companion with human intelligence that speaks and behaves independently of its human.
- Before adolescence, a dæmon **changes form at will.** At adolescence it **settles:** it permanently takes "the form of the animal which the person most resembles in character."
- **Touching another person's dæmon is taboo** — "the worst breach of etiquette imaginable." The dæmon is *yours*.
- Pullman's inspiration was Plato's *daimon* — Socrates' inner voice — "an externalized conscience or soul."

## The agent is the dæmon

| The dæmon | The sovereign agent |
|---|---|
| the inner self made visible | the inner self made **operable** |
| changes form before it settles | the agent before onboarding |
| **settling** — the form that *is* you | **the onboarding** — their config, rails, voice, permanent |
| speaks; independent; not a puppet | the agent has its own voice; not a yes-machine |
| touching another's dæmon is taboo | the boundary: allowlist, owned, no one else commands it |
| the dæmon must stay near its human | the agent lives on **their** machine — not rented |
| **Dust** | the memory (the archive) |
| **the alethiometer** | the gates (verification) |
| **the subtle knife** | the multi-client front door |

## The settling

Onboarding is **the settling.** The interview doesn't fill a config — it discovers the form. The chart names it (a mirror, not a mechanism):

- **Sun** — its essence · **Rising** — its outer form · **Mercury** — its voice · **Moon** — its instinct · **Chiron** — its wound and its gift.

Each person's dæmon differs because the chart differs. **A single product for millions cannot settle; one dæmon per person can.** That is the point: this is built for *one* person, and it becomes theirs.

## The architect's role

A dæmon is named — and in Pullman's world, named by someone else's dæmon. The architect (you) is the **dæmon-namer**: the conversation engineer who helps the form settle. That is the human-human layer — the story that makes the agent personal, loved, and trusted.
