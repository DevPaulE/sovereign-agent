# THE DÆMON FORGE — craft an agent for a person

> Muse and Dots set the terms; we answer them with ownership. Paul's existing ISA primitive *is* the "ideal state = 1 deliverable" — the forge just wraps it in a person.

## What the outsiders do (research, as of 2026-10)

**Meta Muse** — a *personal AI agent*. Powered by the **Muse Wonder** models, which ship two features that matter here:
- **Active Memory** — carries what matters to you across conversations; brings back the relevant details for the task at hand.
- **Active Focus** — keeps working toward your goal when it gets hard, tries other approaches, recovers from errors, **asks for clarification when needed, and checks in before following through on anything important**.

That "checks in before it follows through" is *the same rule as Paul's gate.* Meta sells it as a feature; the forge makes it the architecture.

**OpenAI Dots** — a persistent agent, but **agents-as-a-service**: you subscribe, you don't own. Ksenia Se kept her local OpenClaw instead — the sovereignty counter-position.

## The forge: craft a dæmon for one person

A person brings a **problem** — in their own words, or by uploading files, or by pointing at tools they already use. They already "feed AI stuff"; the forge just gives that feeding a destination.

Crafting their dæmon means setting eight things:

| # | Piece | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Problem** | their words, uploaded — never paraphrased into consultant-speak |
| 2 | **Avatar** | a name and a voice; the dæmon is theirs to speak to |
| 3 | **Ideal state** | **one deliverable** — the single artifact it exists to produce (Paul's `ISA.md`) |
| 4 | **The feed** | files, tools, writing it consumes — as people already do |
| 5 | **Maturation** | memory: the archive + their rails, accumulated over use |
| 6 | **Evolution** | the ideal state upgrades as the person grows — *can* evolve, doesn't drift |
| 7 | **The gate** | outputs verified, not asserted — the "check in before following through" made real |
| 8 | **Ownership** | it runs on *their* box (or theirs when they're ready) — the answer to Dots |

## Why "1 deliverable" is the whole design

An agent with ten goals has none. **One ideal state, one deliverable** is what makes the dæmon legible, gateable, and sellable:

- **Legible** — the person can say what their dæmon is for.
- **Gateable** — you can verify a single artifact; you can't verify "helpfulness."
- **Sellable** — the audit, the install, and the rent all point at the same one thing.

The deliverable can change — that's **evolution** — but there is always exactly one at a time.

## The maturation loop (how it grows without drifting)

```
problem → craft (avatar + ideal state) → feed → the dæmon produces (through the gate)
       → memory records what worked → the ideal state is re-set (evolution) → repeat
```

Maturation is **memory**; evolution is **a re-set ideal state**. Meta's Active Memory is the first; the forge holds both, and the person holds the dæmon.

## The distinction to defend

Muse and Dots are better models. The forge is not competing on the model. It competes on three things they structurally won't give:
1. **Your ideal state** — not their default helpfulness.
2. **Your gate** — verified, not vibes.
3. **Your box** — owned, not rented.

Sell the dæmon, never the model.
