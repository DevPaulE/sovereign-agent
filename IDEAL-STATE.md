# IDEAL STATE — making money

**Set 2026-10-15 by Paul.** The practice's ideal state is **making money**, in the plainest sense: money received, from a client, for the dæmon.

## The single deliverable

**One paying client.** Not a deck, not a plan, not a model, not a repo — **cash in, for work delivered.**

## Why this is the ideal state (and the test it imposes)

Every document in this repo is subordinate to it. The test is brutal and healthy:

- **The gate, the data room, the lead magnet, the lab** — these are the *means*. If they don't produce a paying client, they are hobbies.
- **"Realistic and grounded"** is the constraint: no fantasy revenue, no "build it and they will come," no waiting for the perfect system. The plan must use **what already exists** (the dæmon, the audit, the M6, the warm list, the uncle).
- **The counter-ideal excised:** "make the system perfect first." That's the trap the ideal state forbids.

## The families of evidence (what counts as progress)

1. **A message sent** to a real, warm name. (Not opened, not read — *sent*.)
2. **A reply.** Any reply.
3. **An audit run** on a real person's real folder.
4. **A yes** to the $5,000 install.
5. **Money received.**

Anything else is preparation. **Preparation is not the deliverable.**

## The falsifier

If, after 10 warm messages and 3 audits, there is **no paid install**, the ideal state says the *offer* is wrong — not the market, not the timing, not the product. Change the offer and run it again.
